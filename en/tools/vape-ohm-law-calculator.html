Ohm's Law Formulas for Vaping

Current (I, Amps) = Voltage (V) ÷ Resistance (Ω)
Power (P, Watts) = Voltage² ÷ Resistance = V × I
Regulated Max Amp Draw = Watts ÷ (Cells × 3.2V × 0.90)
⚠️ Battery Safety Note: Always inspect battery wraps for nicks or tears, use trusted authentic cells (Molicel, Sony/Murata, Samsung), and leave at least a 15–20% safety buffer below your battery's true CDR.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do regulated mods draw more amps when the battery is low?
A regulated chip boosts voltage to maintain your set wattage as battery voltage drops from 4.2V down to ~3.2V, which increases current draw (Amps = Watts ÷ Volts).
Does coil resistance change battery amp draw on a regulated mod?
No — on regulated devices, battery current depends on your set wattage, cell count, and battery voltage, not the coil's resistance.
