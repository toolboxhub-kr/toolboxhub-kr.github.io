How the PSA Method Works

The Perfect Sensitivity Approximation (PSA) method uses a binary-narrowing bracket across 7 rounds (multipliers: 0.5/1.5 → 0.6/1.4 → 0.7/1.3 → 0.8/1.2 → 0.85/1.15 → 0.9/1.1 → 0.95/1.05). Each time you pick Lower or Higher, your new center becomes the average of the current base and your choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I test Lower vs Higher sensitivity in-game?
Pick a stationary target or bot in the practice range, strafe left and right continuously, and try to keep your crosshair glued to the target's head. Choose whichever sensitivity causes less over-flicking or jitter.
Does this work for every FPS game?
Yes! The PSA algorithm is completely game-independent and works for Valorant, CS2, Overwatch 2, Apex Legends, Fortnite, and Call of Duty.
