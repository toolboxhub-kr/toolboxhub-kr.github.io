Safety & Steeping Tips

⚠️ Safety First: Always wear nitrile gloves when handling concentrated nicotine, mix in a well-ventilated space, and store all bottles locked away from children and pets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is mixing by weight (grams) recommended?
A 0.01g scale lets you pour directly into your bottle with zero syringe waste or cleanup, giving repeatable accuracy even with thick VG.
How long should DIY e-liquid steep?
Fruit and menthol mixes are often shake-and-vape (1–3 days), whereas custards, creams, and tobaccos taste much smoother after 1–3 weeks in a cool, dark cupboard.
Can I mix 0mg nicotine-free e-liquid?
Yes! Set Target Nicotine to 0 and the calculator will compute only flavors, PG, and VG.
