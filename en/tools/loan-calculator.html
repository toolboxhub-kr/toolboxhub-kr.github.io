Frequently Asked Questions

Why is most of my early payment going toward interest?
Interest is calculated each month on your remaining balance. Because the balance is highest at the start, early payments are interest-heavy and gradually shift toward principal over time.
Does this include property taxes and homeowners insurance?
This calculator shows Principal & Interest (P&I). For total monthly housing cost (PITI), add your local property tax, homeowners insurance, and HOA/PMI if applicable.
Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?
A 15-year mortgage drastically reduces total interest, while a 30-year mortgage keeps mandatory monthly payments lower for cash-flow flexibility.
