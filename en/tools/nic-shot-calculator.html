How the Shortfill & Nic Shot Formula Works

Because adding a 10ml nicotine shot increases the total liquid volume inside your bottle, the exact volume of nicotine shot required is calculated with:

Nic Shot Volume (ml) = (Shortfill ml × Target mg) ÷ (Shot mg - Target mg)
Number of 10ml Shots = Nic Shot Volume ÷ 10
Final Strength (mg/ml) = (Shots × 10 × Shot mg) ÷ (Shortfill ml + Shots × 10)

Frequently Asked Questions

How many nic shots do I add to a 100ml shortfill for 3mg?
Two 10ml 18mg nic shots give 120ml total at exactly 3.0mg/ml.
Why is a 100ml shortfill sold in a 120ml bottle?
Under UK/EU TPD regulations, nicotine-containing bottles are capped at 10ml. Manufacturers fill 100ml of 0mg flavor into a 120ml bottle leaving 20ml of headspace for two 10ml nic shots.
Should I shake my shortfill after adding nic shots?
Yes! Shake vigorously for 60–90 seconds so the nicotine distributes evenly throughout the VG/PG base before filling your tank.
