BMI & Mifflin-St Jeor BMR Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²
Male BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm - 5×age + 5  |  Female BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm - 5×age - 161

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the WHO standard BMI categories?
Below 18.5 is Underweight, 18.5–24.9 is Normal, 25.0–29.9 is Overweight, and 30.0+ is Obese.
Should I eat below my BMR to lose weight faster?
Eating below your BMR for long periods can cause muscle loss and fatigue. Aiming for 300–500 kcal below your TDEE is much more sustainable.
