How Static QR Codes Work

This generator implements the ISO/IEC 18004 QR Code standard directly in JavaScript with Reed-Solomon error correction, encoding your exact data with zero third-party tracking redirects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do QR codes created here ever expire?
No! Because your URL or Wi-Fi credentials are stored directly inside the QR pattern, these codes work forever with unlimited scans.
Which error correction level should I pick?
Level M (15%) is ideal for screens and business cards. Use Q (25%) or H (30%) for outdoor signage that may get scratched or weathered.
Is my Wi-Fi password sent to any server?
No. Everything is generated 100% offline inside your browser.
