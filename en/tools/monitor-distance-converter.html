Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 90 FOV in CS2 wider than 90 FOV in Overwatch 2?
CS2 measures FOV on a 4:3 base (which stretches to 106.26° on 16:9), whereas Overwatch 2 and Valorant measure directly on 16:9 horizontal (103°).
What is the CS2 AWP zoom sensitivity for MDH 0%?
In CS2/CS:GO, setting zoom_sensitivity_ratio 0.818933 matches Monitor Distance 0% for the first AWP zoom level.
