How Browser-Side Image Compression Works

Using the native HTML5 Canvas API, your browser decodes each image locally, scales dimensions proportionally if a max width is set, and re-encodes it into WebP, JPEG, or PNG at your chosen quality factor.

💡 Sweet Spot for Web & Blogs: Convert screenshots and photos to WebP at 80% quality with a max width of 1200px to shrink file sizes by 70%–85% with virtually invisible quality loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. Everything runs strictly in your browser memory — you can even disconnect your internet and it still works.
Why is a PNG output sometimes larger than the original JPG?
PNG uses lossless compression, so converting a lossy JPG to PNG increases file size. Use WebP or JPG when smaller file size is your goal.
Is there a file count or size limit?
No artificial limits — you can batch process multiple photos at once as fast as your device's CPU allows.
