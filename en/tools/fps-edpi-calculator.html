How FPS Sensitivity & cm/360° Conversion Works

Each game engine rotates the camera by a specific degree increment per mouse count, called Yaw (e.g., Valorant = 0.07°, CS2 & Apex = 0.022°, Overwatch 2 = 0.0066°).

eDPI = Mouse DPI × In-Game Sensitivity
cm/360° = [360 ÷ (DPI × Sensitivity × Game Yaw)] × 2.54
Target Sens = Current Sens × (From Yaw ÷ To Yaw) × (From DPI ÷ To DPI)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between eDPI and cm/360?
eDPI only compares sensitivity within the same game. cm/360 measures the actual mousepad distance needed for a full 360° turn, making it universal across every FPS title.
Is 1600 DPI better than 400 or 800 DPI?
Modern sensors show slightly lower motion latency at 1600 DPI with half the in-game sensitivity, while keeping the exact same cm/360 feel.
Why do tactical shooters use lower sensitivity than arena shooters?
Valorant and CS2 reward micro-adjustments for headshots (40–55 cm/360), while Overwatch 2 and Apex require fast 180° turns and vertical tracking (25–40 cm/360).
